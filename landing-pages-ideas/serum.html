§ I, The problem Retinal (retinaldehyde) converts to retinoic acid in one step, versus three for retinol, which is why it acts faster at a lower dose.

Why retinoids usually punish you

Retinoids speed the rate at which skin sheds and rebuilds. Done abruptly, that same acceleration strips the lipids holding the barrier together, the flaking, stinging and redness most people quietly abandon by week two. The active was never the problem. The delivery was.

Meridian encapsulates retinal in a squalane-lecithin shell that releases it gradually overnight, pairing it with panthenol and a 4% dose of niacinamide to reinforce the barrier as turnover climbs. You get the correction without the casualty.

§ III, Method Begin three nights a week. Retinal is photosensitising, pair with daily SPF in the morning.

How to use it

  1. Cleanse and wait

    Dry skin fully. Applying to damp skin drives the retinal in faster than a new user wants.

  2. Three to four drops

    Press, don't rub, across the face at night. One dropper lasts roughly eight weeks.

  3. Seal with moisturiser

    Follow with your cream to lock the squalane cushion in place while you sleep.

  4. Build to nightly

    Add a night each week as tolerated. Most reach nightly use by week four.
