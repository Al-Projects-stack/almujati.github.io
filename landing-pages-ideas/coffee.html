From the roastery floor

The bag on your counter is never more than 48 hours off the drum.

We don't roast to a warehouse. We roast the morning your order prints, then ship it the same day, because freshness is a date, not a slogan.

Most coffee loses its best aromatics within two weeks of roasting, long before it clears a distributor's shelf. Our answer is unfashionably simple: roast nothing until it's sold.

Every lot is sourced direct from fourteen farms, cupped and scored before it earns a profile, then roasted on a 1950s Probat by people who know each origin's sweet spot by ear.

What lands on your doorstep is a dated bag with a one-way valve still venting, proof it was alive this week, not last season.
