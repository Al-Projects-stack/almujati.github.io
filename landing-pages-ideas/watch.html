Health logic

The array is only half the instrument. The on-device model turns raw signal into something that earns its place on your wrist: a warning.

Pulse watches for the patterns that matter and flags them before you'd notice, an irregular rhythm, a downward oxygen trend overnight, a recovery score that quietly tells you to take the day.

  • AF On-device atrial-fibrillation detection with alert log
  • SL Sleep staging and a nightly recovery score
  • FD Fall detection with 30-second emergency SOS
  • PV Privacy by default, raw signal stays on-device
